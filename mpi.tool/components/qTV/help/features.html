Navigation, search and analysis

Contents | Mouse and keyboard controls

Time axis and toolbar

The axis above the picture shows the time from the start of the loaded part of the trace. The thin bar on its top stands for the whole loaded trace with the shown window highlighted; a click on it moves the view there. The toolbar shows the time under the cursor and the bounds of the view in clock ticks and seconds, or, with View > Time units, as the time from the start of the loaded trace, like the axis. H: is the number of elements too narrow to be drawn, C: the number of the shown view in the list found by the last Analyse action.

View history

Every shown view is remembered. View > Back / Forward (Alt+Left / Alt+Right, or the mouse back / forward buttons) step through them. Only the bounds of a view are kept, so the history costs no memory. It is cleared when another trace is loaded and kept on ReLoad.

Bookmarks

Bookmarks > Add bookmark (Ctrl+B) remembers the current view under a name. Bookmarks are stored in the .trace.info.user file next to the trace. Remove bookmark deletes one of them, Remove all bookmarks all of them.

Finding events

  • Find (Ctrl+F) shows the call with a given rank and unique index.
  • View > Find function (Ctrl+Shift+F) looks for the next or previous call of a function by its name, on one rank or on any loaded rank. Calls nested into other calls are found too. Dim the other functions draws all other functions faded while the dialog is open.
  • View > Go to time (Ctrl+G) centers the view at a time given in seconds from the start of the loaded trace.

Selection and details

A left click selects the event under the cursor. It is framed together with the events joined with it by message lines. Show selected with its peers in the right-click menu zooms to fit all of them; Esc drops the selection.

The right-click menu opens windows with details: Info for the event under the cursor, Functions, Messages and User regions for the region dragged with the right button. Every request opens a new window, the earlier ones stay until they are closed or another trace is loaded. A click on a row with a source location opens the source file.

Statistics

Analyse > Statistics of the view, or Statistics in the right-click menu for a dragged region, shows:

  • Functions: number of calls and time per function;
  • Ranks: MPI time per rank, summed over its threads;
  • P2P messages: messages and bytes per pair of sender and receiver.

Calls nested into other calls have rows of their own, marked (nested), and are not added to the MPI time of a rank: their time is a part of the call they are nested into. A call that crosses the bounds of the window adds its part inside the window to the totals.

Analyse

The actions of the Analyse menu look through the loaded trace for the worst cases and open a list of the found views; a click on a row shows it, Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right step through the list.

  • Least BW: messages or collectives with the lowest bandwidth, above a size cut-off.
  • Most Imbalance: collectives or messages whose participants are least in sync.
  • Late sender: the receive (or the wait for it) started before the send, the receiver waited.
  • Late receiver: the send started first and was still running when the receive started, the sender waited.

What is drawn

  • View > Filter hides groups of functions, kinds of messages and communicators.
  • View > Highlight keeps bright the events of a function, of a communicator, or with a message of at least a given size, and dims all the others. The conditions that are set have to hold together; Reset in the dialog clears them.
  • Options > Drawing sets the minimal width of a drawn function, the height of a row (also Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down) and what the colour of an event shows: its kind (the colours of the legend), its function name, its communicator, its duration (from blue for 1 us to red for 1 s) or its message size (from blue for 1 byte to red for 1 GB). Rows lower than a text line show no function names.
  • Options > Legend changes the colours; View > Legend window keeps the colours and their meanings visible beside the trace.
  • Esc cancels a rendering that takes too long. The picture stays incomplete until it is redrawn.
  • Esc cancels a loading as well. What has been read by then stays loaded and is shown; the ranks are cut at different times and the messages may be missing. ReLoad reads the trace again.
  • The time marker, the separators of the ranks and the selection of a range are drawn dark on a light "User Code" colour and light on a dark one.

Pictures and tables

File > Snapshot (F2) saves the picture with its time axis as PNG, JPEG or PDF, by the extension of the file name. The PDF holds the picture as a bitmap. Copy picture (Ctrl+Shift+C) puts it to the clipboard. A right click in any table copies rows or exports the table to a CSV file. A click on a column header sorts the table by that column; numbers are sorted by their values.

Presentation mode

The Presentation button of the toolbar switches to drawing over the current picture: free-hand curve, line, rectangle, ellipse, arrow, text and eraser, with a colour and a pen width. Undo and Redo work on the drawings. The trace cannot be moved or zoomed in this mode; the drawings are dropped when the mode is left, after an offer to save the picture. A saved picture includes the drawings and the time axis.
